About Sharda

Sharda Dental & Homoeopathy Centre is a modern hi-tech, multispeciality dental & homoeopathy centre in Ichalkaranji, Kolhapur District, established in 2006.

Headed by Dr. Amol Daribe (B.D.S.) & Dr. Namrata Amol Daribe (B.H.M.S., C.G.O., C.S.D.), we have a team of super specialists dedicated towards delivering all dental treatments & general health treatment under one roof.

Our hospital is equipped with advanced infrastructure to provide specialised oral health care led by highly qualified & professional doctors. We believe in speciality dental care and our clinic is equipped with all the latest technologies & follows strict sterilization protocols.

The experience, patience, no compromise in quality of the treatment, and ability to take risks are the key factors we are proud of.

"Homoeopathy is supposed to work miracles." — Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

Our homoeopathy practice follows classical principles, offering gentle, side-effect-free treatments for chronic and acute conditions alike — from respiratory ailments to skin diseases, arthritis, and mental health support.
